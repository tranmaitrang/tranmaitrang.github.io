Research themes

Income inequality & multidimensional poverty

Measurement of inequality and poverty in Egypt, Sub-Saharan Africa and Vietnam, and the growth–inequality relationship. The subject of the doctoral dissertation and the 2022 monograph.

11 outputs · 2012–2021

Energy transition & climate policy

Low-carbon pathways, rooftop solar investment decisions, electric-vehicle policy, climate risk insurance, and national action plans aligned with Vietnam's net-zero commitment.

9 outputs · 2021–2025

FDI, trade & economic integration

FDI–emissions–growth linkages under VECM and ARDL specifications, FTA effects on agricultural exporters, and Vietnam's integration into the global economy.

8 outputs · 2010–2024

Economic resilience

Threshold and machine-learning approaches to how Vietnamese output and exports absorb shocks — currently the main line of work, using industry-level panel data from 2000 onward.

Active line · 2026–

Firm competitiveness & innovation

Determinants of SME competitiveness, provincial innovation activity, and the drivers of the circular-economy transition among Vietnamese enterprises.

4 outputs · 2022–2025

Public services, PPP & environmental governance

Public–private partnership in urban wastewater, decentralised environmental management, rural solid waste, and public-service delivery in a socialist-oriented market economy.
